About Us

Crown Steel Welding & Fabrication is a steel and glass workshop on Cogam Road, Muddavaddi, in Saligao, Bardez. The work covers staircases, main gates, balcony glass, gold-finish railings, name boards and roofing for houses and shops.

Send a photo, a rough width and the place where it will be fitted. The workshop will say what can be made, then cut, weld and fit it on site in Goa.

Services

Staircases and railings

Straight and turned staircases in mild steel and stainless steel, built to the floor height.

Main gates and grills

Swing and sliding gates, window grills and compound steelwork for the front of the house.

Balcony glass

Glass panels on steel or stainless posts for balconies and stair openings.

Name boards

Cut-letter name boards and shopfront letters in steel.

Roofing and shades

Roofing sheets, shades and roof structures for terraces and open areas.

Custom fabrication

Doors, grills and other steel pieces made to a size you send.

Products

Grills, roofing, staircases, balcony glass, balcony railings, main gates, sheds, gold railings, chairs, and showpieces.
